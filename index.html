Shinagawa-ku and Tokyo subsidies and support

The subsidies, allowances and support payments Shinagawa-ku offers its residents, and the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's that they can claim too, summarised in English, with the Japanese name of each and a link to the official page where there is one. A snapshot as at 16 November 2026: amounts and dates change, so check the ward's page before applying.

Closing soon

  • Electricity and gas support, ¥4,000 per household （しながわ電気・ガス料金緊急支援事業）. Every household registered on 1 June 2026, with no income limit. The main deadline (31 October) has passed, but paper applications postmarked by 20 November 2026 are accepted. Call centre: 0120-667-621. Ward page
  • Living support card, ¥5,000 per person （しながわ生活応援事業）. A Vanilla Visa card （うれしなカード） sent without application to everyone registered on 1 January 2026, to be spent by 31 January 2027. A card that never arrived can be collected at the ward counter from 1 December 2026 to 7 January 2027, by booking in advance. Ward page

Where the ward publishes this

There is no single list. The sources are:

  1. The ward website's topic sections, the main source:
    • 子どもの手当・助成: child allowance, medical subsidies, single-parent support
    • 住まい・生活: household payments, housing grants, premium gift vouchers
    • 高齢者 (seniors), 障害のある方 (disability), 生活にお困りの方 (hardship), 就業支援 (employment)
  2. しながわこども未来ガイド: every family programme by life stage, from pregnancy to university; the best single overview for families.
  3. The annual housing guide （住宅施策のご案内, PDF）.
  4. Rules documents （要綱）, which give each grant's exact terms: who qualifies, what is covered and how much.
  5. 手続きナビ, which shows the procedures a life event triggers and takes many applications online.
  6. The ward's official LINE account and 広報しながわ (the ward newsletter), which announce one-off payments first. In 2026 the ¥4,000 payment could be applied for, and collected the same day, through LINE.
  7. Tokyo Metropolitan Government programmes listed on the ward's pages but run by Tokyo, such as the 018サポート child payment and the Silver Pass for over-70s.

What is available

Children and families

  • Child allowance （児童手当）, with no income limit since October 2024; children's medical costs covered （子どもすこやか医療費助成）.
  • Free at the ward's public schools for every family, regardless of income: school lunches (since 2023), the Year 9 school trip (since 2025) and junior high uniforms (from April 2026).
  • For new parents: free monthly nappy deliveries for babies under one （見守りおむつ定期便）, post-natal care stays and visits （産後ケア）, grants towards post-natal home help （産後ドゥーラ）, babysitter support, and four pregnancy ultrasound scans paid for.
  • Moving within the ward with children （子育てファミリー世帯転居費用助成）: up to ¥300,000 towards the costs of moving into a home bought, or ¥150,000 for a private rental, plus ¥20,000 for three or more children. It requires two years' residence in the ward, a contract signed on or after 1 April 2026, and an application within six months of moving. Ward page
  • University scholarships that need not be repaid （給付型大学奨学金）, and high-school loans.
  • Children use ward facilities free since 1 July 2026 （子どもの施設使用料無料化事業）.

Home and energy

  • Zero-carbon grant （しながわゼロカーボンアクション助成）, open until 15 March 2027 or until the budget runs out. It covers solar panels, home batteries, Ecocute and hybrid water heaters, Ene-Farm, efficient air conditioners and fridges, parcel boxes, zero-energy homes and zero-emission cars: half the cost or a fixed sum. Environment division: 03-5742-6949. 2026 pamphlet (PDF)
  • Home improvement grant （住宅改善工事助成） for energy-saving and accessibility work: closed for 2026; the next round is expected around April 2027. It has paid 10% of the cost, up to ¥200,000, for households with income up to ¥12m. Housing division: 03-5742-6776.
  • Earthquake-proofing: a free seismic survey, and up to ¥650,000 towards a reinforced shelter room. One pamphlet says these run until the 2027 financial year; not confirmed as still open.
  • Grants for condominium management associations （マンション支援）.
  • Premium gift vouchers: pay a set price for more spending value in local shops, in paper or digital form, on sale in spring and autumn.

Health and seniors

  • Hearing aids for over-65s, with no income limit.
  • Smartphone purchases for seniors.
  • Grants for securing furniture against earthquakes.
  • Pregnancy dental check-ups and fertility treatment.
  • The 2026 vaccination subsidies (shingles, flu) were not found; check the ward's vaccination page.

Hardship and single parents

Child-rearing allowance, single-parent medical costs, help with rent and housing, study support and welfare loans, all linked from the children's allowances section.

Tokyo Metropolitan Government programmes

Residents of Shinagawa can claim these as well as the ward's, and several stack with them. Tokyo collects its own programmes, about 800 of them, in Tokyo支援ナビ (a link to the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's site), searchable by category and life stage.

Children and families

  • 018 Support （018サポート）: ¥5,000 a month (up to ¥60,000 a year) for every resident aged 0 to 18, with no income limit, paid in August, December and April. A late application is paid back to the start of the year. Call centre: 0120-056-018. Official page
  • Child-rearing support plus （子育て応援＋）: ¥11,000 once for each child aged 0 to 14, paid without application between February 2026 and April 2027.
  • Baby First Gift （赤ちゃんファーストギフト）: a gift card for childcare goods for each baby born in Tokyo, applied for together with 018 Support. Its value has risen for births from January 2026; check the amount on the official page.
  • Private high-school fees （私立高等学校等授業料軽減助成金）: with the national grant, up to ¥501,000 a year (the Tokyo average fee) for students living in Tokyo, with no income limit since 2024. The national grant is applied for in June and Tokyo's in July. 2026 announcement

Everyone

  • Tokyo App living-support points （東京アプリ生活応援事業）: 11,000 Tokyo Points for each resident aged 15 or over with a My Number card, through the Tokyo App. They are not given automatically: apply by 1 April 2027. The points last 24 months and can be exchanged for other points, such as PayPay. Official page

Home and energy

  • Tokyo Zero-Emission Points （東京ゼロエミポイント）: a discount at the till when replacing an air conditioner, fridge, water heater or lighting with an efficient model at a participating shop, until 31 March 2027. Official site
  • Home batteries （家庭における蓄電池導入促進事業）: Tokyo's Clean Energy Agency (クール・ネット東京) subsidises home battery systems, new or existing homes, per kWh installed. It can be combined with Shinagawa's zero-carbon grant; check each programme's rules on claiming both.

Closed or not found

  • The ¥20,000 per child price-rise payment （物価高対応子育て応援手当） closed on 20 April 2026.
  • The bicycle-helmet grant appears to have ended in March 2025.
  • No current grant was found for earthquake circuit breakers （感震ブレーカー）, though the ward hands out furniture-securing fixtures free at its disaster-prevention section （防災課） and local community centres （地域センター）.
